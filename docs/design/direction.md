# Art direction: Reading room

Chosen by the user on 2026-11-03 from three mockups dealt by design-taste-frontend (app-screen run).

- **Style:** `editorial-greeting`, light, seed 1285963718. Full sheet: `.taste-skill/app-editorial/direction.json`.
- **Redraws:** none.
- **Name:** Stint (working name, a stretch of focused work).

## The move
- The greeting is the h1. It's set in Instrument Serif in a centred column on a 12-column grid, with spans that change by row but share column lines.
- Every figure below the greeting is set at regular weight on tonal panels. Each panel is one layer, with no tray-inside-tray.

## Its face (chosen)
1. **The greeting names today's one fact from the data**, never "Welcome back". For example: "Good evening, Abdallah" with the line "Calculus II midterm in 9 days. About 1.5 h a day gets you there."
2. **A surface from the product's world:** a pale sage paper panel (`#f3f5f2`) in place of `#f9f9f9`, with white kept as the page ground.

## Signature element
**The stamina ladder.** It has 8 rungs (25, 28, 31, 34, 37, 41, 45 and 50 min), with the reached rungs in teal and 3 pips for rounds completed at the current rung. It appears on Today beside the start block, and in Stats with its history.

## Colour
- **One confident accent: deep teal.** It goes on the single primary action (Start studying / Back to it) and on today's main data: today's ring, the current week's bar and the reached ladder rungs. It is under 2% of pixels outside the Focus screen.
- **Subject colours** are identity, not data. They stay pale and appear as dots and icons. They're used as bar colours only in "time per subject".
- **Light:** white ground and sage panels.
- **Dark:** a green-black ground with the teal lifted. It's designed separately, not inverted (see `system.md`).
- **Banned:** gradients, glass, glow, icons in tinted squares, emoji, violet or indigo, and cream with terracotta.

## Type
- **Instrument Serif:** the greeting and the Focus timer digits. These are the two moments where the app speaks.
- **Instrument Sans (400/500/600):** everything else, with tabular figures on every number.
- **Every figure is set at 400.** Sentence case throughout, with no tracked capitals.

## Shell
- **Open plane:** a labelled sidebar of 224px on the page ground, with a neutral grey pill for the active item and no top bar.
- **Phone:** a bottom tab bar of five destinations instead of a menu sheet, because the student switches between screens often.
